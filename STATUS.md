| Function-field double cover (G07a) | The function field of the family curve is `ℂ(t)[w]/(w² − h)`, `t = X/Y`, with `h` squarefree of degree `2m+1`; degree two over `ℂ(t)`. Its elements integral over `ℂ[t]` are exactly `a + b·w`, `a, b ∈ ℂ[t]` (G07b-1); that closure is Dedekind and equals `ℂ[t][W]/(W² − h)`, whose maximal ideals are exactly the points `(c,d)` with `d² = h(c)` (G07b-2a/2b). Places containing `ℂ[t]` correspond bijectively to these points: two over `t = c` when `h(c) ≠ 0`, one when `h(c) = 0` (G07b-2c). Exactly one further place lies over `t = ∞`, found through the isomorphism with the conjugate family's chart (G07b-3). With branch points defined by fibre count (recorded choice), there are exactly `2m+2`: `∞` and the `2m+1` roots of `h` (G08). Its differentials `Ω[K⁄ℂ]` form a one-dimensional `K`-vector space spanned by `dt`, with `2w·dw = h'(t)·dt` (G09a-1). At a local ring with principal maximal ideal `(u)` and residue field generated by the constants, the differentials are generated by `du`, so they do not depend on the choice of `u` (G09a-2a, generic); every point place of the cover is such a ring, with residue field `ℂ` (G09a-2b). Over the function field this gives, at each point place, that `du` is a nonzero spanning differential and that two uniformizers give coefficients differing by a unit, so an order at the place is independent of the uniformizer (G09a-2c). The same holds at the place over `t = ∞`, through the chart isomorphism of G07b-3 made into a `ℂ`-algebra isomorphism (G09a-2d). At a point place with `h(c) ≠ 0`, `t − c` generates the maximal ideal and its differential is `dt` (G09a-2e). At a ramified point place (`h(c) = 0`) the root `w` generates it, `h'(t)` is a unit there, and `h'(t)·(f·dt) = (2·f·w)·dw`, so `dt` picks up one factor of the uniformizer (G09a-2f). At the place over `t = ∞`, read through the chart map `t ↦ 1/s`, `dt` is `−2w'·dw'` over `s²·h'(s)` with `h'(s)` a unit and `s = w'²·(unit)`, which is one factor of the uniformizer against four (G09a-2g). Regularity of `f·dt` at a point place is proved equivalent to `f` being in the local ring when `h(c) ≠ 0`, and to `f·w` being in it when `h(c) = 0` (G09b-1). Regularity at infinity is defined through the chart isomorphism (G09b-2), and is equivalent to `φ(f)/w'³` lying in the conjugate local ring, a triple zero (G09b-3a). The holomorphic differentials form a `ℂ`-subspace, regular at every place (G09b-3b), containing the `m` linearly independent `tⁱ·dt/w`, `i < m` (G09b-3c). A differential regular at every point place is `(a/w + b)·dt` with `a, b ∈ ℂ[t]` (G09b-3d-1); the triple zero at infinity forces `b = 0` and `deg a < m` (G09b-3d-2), so the `tⁱ·dt/w` are a basis and **the genus is `m`** (`family_genus`, G09b-3d-3), read as the dimension of the holomorphic differentials of the function field. Not formalized: the Riemann–Hurwitz degree identity as a divisor statement, ramification indices, a `ℤ`-valued order. |

## Internal proved machinery

- Cartesian/complex coordinate equivalence, polynomial divisibility from an
  infinite real locus, actual isometry interfaces and a common rotation center.
- Sharp examples, weight restrictions, equality normal form and normalization
  of its parameters by similarity (not normalization of an algebraic curve).
- Family polynomial irreducibility, degree and infinitude; four-form coefficient
  filters; chart Jacobians, singular pair and global transport under ambient maps.
- Affine spectrum density, all boundary-chart closure identities, inversion
  homeomorphisms, four open projective charts, atlas uniqueness and projective
  irreducibility. These are proved ingredients, not a genus theorem.
- The genus of any field over `ℂ` without a chosen model (R01a,
  `FunctionFieldGenus`): places as valuation subrings, regular and holomorphic
  differentials, and invariance of the genus under `ℂ`-algebra isomorphisms
  (`genus_congr`). For the family it agrees with G09's explicit places, so the
  function field of the family curve has genus `m` in this sense (R01b,
  `familyFunctionField_genus`).
- The Kummer cover `yⁿ = f` for squarefree `f` (R01c-1, `KummerField`):
  irreducibility, the coordinate ring inside the function field, and the
  differentials `Ω = K·dx` with `n·yⁿ⁻¹·dy = f'(x)·dx`. The quartic
  `X⁴ + Y⁴ = 2` is the case `n = 4`, `f = 2 − x⁴`.
- On the quartic, `dx/y³`, `x·dx/y³` and `dx/y²` are holomorphic and
  independent (R01d, `FermatHolomorphic`), so its holomorphic differentials
  have dimension at least three, exactly three by R01e-3.
- A similarity between real loci induces an isomorphism of the function fields
  (R01f, `similarityFunctionFieldEquiv`); the quartic's function field is its
  Kummer field (`fermatFunctionFieldAlgEquiv`).
- The Kummer ring `ℂ[x][Y]/(Yⁿ − f)` for squarefree `f`: maximal ideals are the
  points, each local ring is a DVR with an explicit uniformizer, and the ring
  is Dedekind (R01c-2a, `KummerLocal`). Its places over the affine line, their
  local differentials and the regularity criteria for `F·dx` (R01c-2b,
  `KummerPlaces`).
- The quartic's chart at infinity: `x = 1/s`, `y = r/s` with `r⁴ = 2s⁴ − 1`
  identifies its function field with a second Kummer field, and at a place
  over `x = ∞` the differential `F·dx` is regular iff `φ(F)/s²` lies in the
  local ring (R01e-1, `FermatInfinity`).
- A holomorphic differential of the quartic is
  `(a₀(x) + a₁(x)·y + a₂(x)·y² + a₃(x)·y³)·dx/y³` with polynomial `aⱼ`, and each
  of the four pieces is holomorphic on its own, by the automorphism `y ↦ i·y`
  (R01e-2, `FermatSplit`).
- The quartic's genus is exactly three (R01e-3, `FermatGenus`): at the place
  over `x = ∞` a holomorphic piece `aⱼ(x)·yʲ·dx/y³` with `aⱼ ≠ 0` forces
  `deg aⱼ + j ≤ 1`, so `dx/y³`, `x·dx/y³` and `dx/y²` span the holomorphic
  differentials (`quartic_holomorphicSpace_eq_span`, `quartic_genus`,
  `fermatFunctionField_genus`).
