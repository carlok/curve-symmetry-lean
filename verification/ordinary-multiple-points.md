## Definitions

OrdinaryMultiplePoints uses standard algebraic notions on `BPoly`:

- `pointIdeal x y` is the ideal generated by `X-x` and `Y-y`.
  `mem_pointIdeal_iff` proves that membership is exactly vanishing at the point.
- `HasMultiplicityAt P x y n` means `P` is in the `n`th power of that ideal and
  not in the `(n+1)`st. `hasMultiplicityAt_unique` proves it determines `n`.
- `OrdinaryAtOrigin P n` means multiplicity `n` at the origin, together with a
  degree-`n` homogeneous component equal to a nonzero constant times `n`
  nonzero, pairwise nonproportional linear forms. Nonproportionality is a
  nonzero 2x2 determinant for each pair.

No definition selects the family or its points.

## Results

- `jacobianSingular_of_mem_sq`: a second-order zero is a Jacobian singularity.
  `hasMultiplicityAt_one` gives multiplicity one at every nonsingular zero.
- `binaryForm_eq_prod`: `aX^m+bY^m = a * prod (X - zeta^i w Y)` with
  `w^m=-b/a`, using Mathlib's Kummer factorization and polynomial functional
  equality.
- `fourTermForm_ordinary`: every four-term chart equation with nonzero low
  coefficients is ordinary `m`-fold at its origin, for `m>0`.
- `family_mixed_chart_nonsingular`: the mixed chart equation has no Jacobian
  singularity, derived from the global critical-locus classification.
- `family_ordinary_multiple_points`: for `m>=2` and nonreal `alpha`, the affine
  and reciprocal origins are ordinary `m`-fold; every other zero of those chart
  equations, and every zero of both mixed chart equations, has multiplicity one.

With `fourTermForm_tangent_data` (the cones are the displayed binary forms) and
`family_diagonal_tangent_direction_counts`, this closes G06.

## Scope boundary

Multiplicity is computed in the chart polynomial ring. `(0,0)` lies only in
the affine standard chart and `(∞,∞)` only in the reciprocal one. The package
does not prove invariance of multiplicity under the nonlinear inversion
transitions or under ambient automorphisms, and it constructs no local ring,
normalization, delta invariant or genus. G07–G09 and R01 remain open.
