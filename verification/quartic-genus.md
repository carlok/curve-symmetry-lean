# The quartic comparison (R01) — 2026-09-29

Remark 5 of the note: `Re(z⁴) = 1` attains the rotation bound in degree four
but is not in the `m = 2` family, "since its normalization has genus three
rather than two". User decisions: the proof goes through genus as printed
(2026-09-15), and the row closes only with the quartic's genus exactly three
(2026-09-28).

G09 proved the family's genus through its explicit list of places
(`holomorphicDifferentials m α`). A similarity only gives an isomorphism of
function fields, so R01 needs the genus stated without a chosen model. The
packages, in order: R01a (this note's first section), R01b family bridge,
R01c-1 Kummer field, R01d three holomorphic differentials of the quartic,
R01f similarity to isomorphism, R01c-2 Kummer places, R01e genus exactly 3.

## R01a: the genus of a field over `ℂ`

[FunctionFieldGenus](../lean/FunctionFieldGenus.lean), generic in a field `K`
with `[Algebra ℂ K]`.

| Notion | Lean | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| place | `IsComplexPlace O` | `O : ValuationSubring K`, `O ≠ ⊤`, contains `algebraMap ℂ K z` for every `z` |
| regular at `O` | `regularAt O` | the `ℂ`-span of the `a • D b` with `a, b ∈ O` |
| holomorphic | `holomorphicSpace K` | regular at every place |
| genus | `genus K` | `Module.finrank ℂ (holomorphicSpace K)` |

`mem_regularAt_iff_of_generator`: if `u ∈ O` and every `db`, `b ∈ O`, is
`g·du` with `g ∈ O`, then `ω` is regular at `O` exactly when `ω = f·du` with
`f ∈ O`, the criterion G09 used at each place.
`exists_D_eq_smul_of_span_eq_top` produces that generator condition from a
ring `A → K` whose own differentials are generated by `du`, which is what
G09a-2b proved for the point places.

Coefficients are taken in `K`: an `O`-module structure on `Ω[K⁄ℂ]` brings
back the instance diamond met in G09a-2c.

Invariance: `kaehlerTransportEquiv e` upgrades `kaehlerTransport` (G09b-2)
along `e : K ≃ₐ[ℂ] L` to a `ℂ`-linear equivalence (inverse checked on the
`D a`, which span `Ω[K⁄ℂ]` over `K`). Places pull back to places
(`IsComplexPlace.comap`), the transport carries `regularAt (O.comap e)` into
`regularAt O` (`kaehlerTransport_mem_regularAt`), so it carries holomorphic
differentials to holomorphic ones (`kaehlerTransport_mem_holomorphicSpace`;
with `L = K`, automorphisms preserve them). `holomorphicSpace_map`,
`holomorphicSpaceEquiv` and **`genus_congr : genus K = genus L`** follow.

Discreteness of the places is not assumed. For the family, and later for the
quartic, the place classifications show every place is a discrete valuation
ring; R01b proves that `holomorphicSpace` agrees with G09's
`holomorphicDifferentials` there.
